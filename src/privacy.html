隐私政策

中文

一句话概括：这是一款本地优先的 HushJSON。您的所有数据都留在您自己的设备上，我们什么都不收集。

数据收集

我们不收集、不传输、不存储任何个人信息或用户内容到外部服务器。您输入或粘贴的全部 JSON 内容、格式化与校验处理，均完全在您自己的设备本地完成，永远不会发送给我们或任何第三方。

本地存储

应用可能在您的设备本地保存格式化偏好（如主题、缩进设置）以及您选择保存的历史记录。这些数据仅存储在您的设备上，不会离开您的设备，我们与任何外部服务均无法访问。

第三方服务

应用不集成任何第三方统计、广告、崩溃上报或追踪 SDK。核心格式化功能不发起任何网络请求。

儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何用户数据（包括儿童数据），适合所有年龄段的用户使用。

联系方式

如对隐私政策有任何疑问，欢迎在 GitHub 仓库提交 Issue：github.com/sky-jiangcheng/hush-json/issues

政策变更

我们可能不定期更新本政策，任何变更都会发布在本页面，并同步更新下方生效日期。

生效日期：2026-07-19

English

In short: this is a local-first JSON formatting tool. All of your data stays on your own device — we collect nothing.

Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information or user content on external servers. All JSON input, formatting, and validation occur entirely on your own device. Nothing you type or paste is ever sent to us or to any third party.

Local Storage

The app may store your formatting preferences (such as theme and indent settings) and any history you choose to keep. This data is kept locally on your device only. It never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any external service.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. There are no network calls made by the core formatting features.

Children

The app does not collect any data from any user, including children, and is suitable for users of all ages.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: github.com/sky-jiangcheng/hush-json/issues

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the effective date below will be updated accordingly.

Effective date: 2026-07-19
